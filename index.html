Patan Durbar Square, Nepal // 4,300 ft

The Beauty of Pagoda Architecture

Temples and historic monuments across the Kathmandu valley have been built in the traditional architectural styles of the Newa people. Just as such, the exquisite royal palace complex of the Patan Durbar Square reflects the fusion of ancient Vajrayana Buddhism and Hinduism with the Pagoda style of architecture.

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Durbar Square is today a museum, where the culture of the local people has been preserved in a beautiful state. The museum contains idols, jewelry, coinage, scriptures and various other objects of artistic and historical importance.
