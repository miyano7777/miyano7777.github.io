Chitwan National Park, Nepal // 100 FT

Humans and Rhinos: One Coexistence

Words and photos by Nischal Kunwar

A living landscape

In the southern plains of Nepal, resides a fierce creature with skin tougher than metal armor, a magnificent singular horn atop its snout, and a sense of smell and hearing greater than excellent. I had the blessed opportunity to view this beautiful animal, while in Chitwan National Park, back home in Nepal.

In Sauhara, Chitwan, the streets are ruled not just by cars or bikes, but also by the natives: the Rhinos…

I visited the Chitwan National Park in January, 2026 with friends that I hold closest to my heart. It was my first such trip outside the Kathmandu valley, my home, where it was just us little kids (however, not so little) without any actual adult supervision. We never had imagined something like that would be possible, not at all. Even today, when me and my friends discuss the trip, we humorously wonder however we sought permissions, and more importantly, the money for that journey. Nevertheless, it was one of the most memorable things I have done in my life of 19 years. If there is one place I wish to go back to, it would be that hammock, from which I hopelessly fell, at the hotel in Sauraha. The visions of the wonderfully lit streets of the town still are tattooed all so well in my mind.

Rhinos, in Sauraha, are the sovereigns. Unfortunately today an endangered species, the One-Horned Rhinoceros is one of nature’s most exquisite creations. As these animals wander the forests and streets of Chitwan, it seems as though there can indeed occur a beautiful coexistence between humans and animals.

Inside the national park, we saw very many gorgeous flora and fauna. As we rowed on a boat through a small river, we saw the spotted deer, the Gharial crocodiles, Asiatic elephants, peacocks, along with many others. While we walked through ruins of an old hotel, we heard the beautiful songs of various birds. It was as though one was one with nature.

Chitwan National Park is a location I would suggest to anyone enthusiastic about nature and biodiversity. If they wish to get a clear understanding of how the South Asian air treats its very unique animal and aquatic kingdoms, Chitwan National Park, in fact, the whole of Nepal, is a brilliant place to explore.

Written on 27 November, 2026
